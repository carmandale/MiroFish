> Audience: leadership

> Standard: every recommendation below is tied to `internal-primary` or verified external evidence. Where the evidence is thinner, it is labeled as inference.

## Executive Read

The immediate problem is not lack of strategic options. It is a cash emergency inside a structurally unprofitable operating picture.

- `specs/004-real-growth-research/artifacts/transform/qbo_08.md` shows only `$19,455` in cash, `0` months of runway, and monthly burn of roughly `$397K`.
- The same `qbo_08.md` artifact also shows `netIncome=-608991.8` and `netMarginPct=-8.6`, so leadership should not treat this as a collections-only problem.
- `specs/004-real-growth-research/artifacts/transform/qbo_07.md` shows `$242,641` in AR, with the top five balances totaling roughly `$233,716`.
- `specs/004-real-growth-research/artifacts/transform/pd_09.md` shows a market pattern where large deals die internally at scale, so "just chase bigger new deals" is not an immediate rescue plan.

The near-term goal should be simple: create cash, reduce burn where possible, convert the warmest existing-account opportunities, and avoid betting the next 90 days on slow or speculative lanes.

Why:
- The fastest cash on the board is already invoiced.
- `qbo_07.md` shows the top balances are concentrated enough for direct executive follow-up to matter.

Priority balances:
- `25062 ASU STEM Innovation Lab Apps` — `$97,500`
- `26003 Adobe Mantra Machine` — `$77,816`

- `25061 IVRY VET1 Unity Update (Phase 1)` — `$23,400`
- `24077 Engineering Data Visualization and Digital Art Service Agreement` — `$10,000`

Leadership action:
- Treat the next 10 business days as a cash-recovery sprint with owner-level outreach and explicit collection dates.

### 2. Concentrate new selling on warm existing-account expansion, not broad net-new hunting

Why:
- `pd_03.md` and `pd_04.md` show real relationship depth with repeat buyers.
- The strongest open-value relationship accounts already have history, contacts, or both.

Accounts to prioritize:
- `FGPG` — `open_value=$1,350,000` in `pd_04.md`, but treat this as a high-variance upside case because FGPG's historical won value is only `$367,389`; the open pipe is far larger than prior realized revenue.
- `Angelo State University` — `open_value=$510,000` in `pd_03.md` and `pd_04.md`
- `160over90` — `open_value=$294,000` in `pd_03.md` and `pd_04.md`
- `Freelance Producer` — `open_value=$450,000` in `pd_04.md` but lower strategic confidence because the relationship is less institutionally grounded

Leadership action:
- For the next 30 days, require that every large pursuit be categorized as either `existing-account expansion`, `adjacent referral`, or `true net-new`.
- Default scarce executive time toward the first two categories.
- Separate "high-confidence expansion" from "relationship-adjacent whale bets" so leadership attention does not get consumed by speculative upside.

### 3. Pair the collections sprint with a fast profitability review

Why:
- `qbo_08.md` shows the company is not just cash-starved; it is running at negative net margin.
- Even perfect AR collection would not cover a full month of current burn.

Leadership action:
- In the same 10-business-day window as collections, review discretionary spend, contractor load, and any active work that is unlikely to convert to near-term cash.
- Treat this as an operating-margin correction sprint, not only a receivables sprint.

### 4. Turn maintenance/support into a named cash-defense offer right now

Why:
- `~/dev/quickbooks/data-package/13-groove-jones-capabilities.md` shows maintenance/support has the best win rate in the company (`73%`) and `$1.61M` of historical revenue.
- This is the clearest internal evidence for recurring or semi-recurring work that does not depend on full custom-project resets.

Leadership action:
- Package a named support offer around OS updates, device compatibility, analytics review, content refresh, and light operational support.
- Offer it first to accounts already visible in `pd_03.md` and `pd_04.md`, especially those with high historical revenue and multiple relationships.

### 5. Keep healthcare moving, but do not ask it to save the next 90 days

Why:
- `fc_05.md` shows `J&J Ottava` remains one of the largest active projects.
- `ext-002-healthcare-medtech-signals.md` makes healthcare one of the strongest evidence-backed adjacent lanes.
- But this is still a strategic growth lane, not a guaranteed short-cycle cash fix.

Leadership action:
- Protect and expand current healthcare relationships.
- Do not let long-cycle healthcare BD crowd out collections and maintenance packaging.

### 6. Do not treat defense capture as a rescue plan

Why:
- `ext-003-defense-training-signals.md` proves the market is real.
- It does not prove that Groove Jones can convert a defense opportunity fast enough to solve the present cash crisis.

Leadership action:
- Keep defense as a partner-led strategic lane, not the operating assumption for the next quarter.
