# Groove Jones Strategy Lab Decision Memo

> Date: 2026-03-21
>
> Basis: live Strategy Lab run `proj_157dbd7d089a` plus the spec-004 research bundle

## Decision

Groove Jones should run the business on a two-speed strategy:

1. use `Existing Client Maintenance + Expansion` as the immediate operating priority for the next `30-90 days`
2. build `Healthcare / Pharma Training Growth` as the primary adjacent growth lane for the next `6-24 months`
3. keep `Defense / Government Entry` alive as a partner-led option lane, not the main operating thesis

This is the clearest synthesis of the live Strategy Lab run and the strongest available research in the current workspace.

## Why

### Immediate operating reality

The company is in a cash-constrained position and should not treat long-cycle strategic bets as the answer to the next quarter.

The strongest immediate logic comes from the `30-90-day-triage.md` brief:

- cash is critically low
- burn remains structurally high
- receivables are concentrated enough for executive intervention to matter
- speculative net-new pursuits are weaker near-term rescue mechanisms than current-account recovery and expansion

That makes existing-account expansion and maintenance/support the most practical near-term move, even if it is not the most elegant strategic story.

### Medium-term growth logic

The strongest adjacent growth thesis is healthcare / pharma training and procedural understanding.

Why this lane stands out:

- external evidence is stronger here than for most other adjacent lanes
- Groove Jones already has relevant past-performance credibility
- the buyer logic is concrete: training quality, procedural confidence, workflow value, and regulated learning environments
- the lane is more durable than events/agency work and faster to credibly enter than defense

### Why defense stays secondary

Defense is real and strategically attractive, but the live report correctly treats it as slower and heavier:

- lower near-term time to cash
- higher compliance and qualification friction
- stronger fit as a partner-led option than as the primary revenue rescue thesis

## What We Do Now

### Next 30 days

- run an executive collections sprint on top receivables
- prioritize warm existing-account expansion ahead of broad net-new hunting
- package a named maintenance/support offer around:
  - OS updates
  - device compatibility
  - analytics review
  - content refresh
  - light operational support
- keep healthcare/pharma relationship development moving, but do not expect it to solve the next 90 days

### Next 90 days

- convert selected current accounts into repeatable maintenance/support or refresh retainers
- separate true expansion opportunities from speculative whale pursuits
- build a sharper healthcare/pharma training offer with:
  - proof assets
  - reference language
  - procurement/compliance framing
  - measurable-outcome positioning

### Next 6-24 months

- make healthcare/pharma training the main adjacent growth lane
- use maintenance/support as the bridge from episodic project churn into more durable recurring revenue
- pursue defense selectively through partner-first and small-business-accessible pathways

## What We Should Not Do

- do not ask defense capture to rescue the next quarter
- do not build the strategy around broad AI/XR positioning claims
- do not treat events/agency work as the core future unless it is tightly filtered to high-value healthcare/medtech relationships
- do not mistake interesting market narratives for an operating cash plan

## What Would Change This Decision

- proof that true net-new materially outperforms existing-account expansion in near-term conversion
- stronger verified evidence that sports/events work is more durable than the current workspace suggests
- a concrete defense partner path with near-term qualified opportunities
- better staffing/capacity evidence showing Groove Jones can support heavier healthcare or defense expansion without delivery strain

## Bottom Line

If leadership needs one sentence:

Stabilize the base through current-client expansion and support now, then build healthcare/pharma training as the main durable growth lane, while keeping defense alive as a slower partner-led option.
