> Date: 2026-03-21
>
> Scope: top two lanes from live Strategy Lab run `proj_157dbd7d089a`

## Bottom Line

The Strategy Lab recommendation is directionally good, but the evidence quality is uneven.

- `Existing Client Maintenance + Expansion` is probably the correct immediate operating call, but it is supported mostly by internal synthesis and operating judgment rather than strong independent triangulation.
- `Healthcare / Pharma Training Growth` is the strongest genuinely evidence-backed strategic lane because it combines verified external signals with relevant Groove Jones past-performance fit.

## Lane 1: Existing Client Maintenance + Expansion

### Why the recommendation makes sense

The live report ranks this lane first because it best matches the current operating problem:

- highest time-to-cash
- highest durability in the report
- strongest practical fit with what Groove Jones already does
- lowest required investment

This is a sensible call in a cash emergency.

### What supports it

The strongest citations behind this lane come from:

- `30-90-day-triage.md`
- `6-24-month-strategy.md`
- the lane template in `lane_templates.json`
- the narrative interview transcript

The most compelling concrete support is the triage brief:

- it explicitly says the fastest cash is already invoiced
- it argues for current-account conversion and collections over speculative new-logo pursuit
- it ties the recommendation to AR concentration, current financial stress, and relationship depth

### What is weak

This lane is still more self-referential than ideal.

Weaknesses:

- the core recommendation is heavily backed by leadership synthesis docs produced from the same workspace
- `capability_fit` is backed by the lane template rather than direct measured performance data
- `evidence_strength` is backed by the narrative interview transcript, which is the weakest evidence layer in the stack
- the current report does not independently prove that existing-account expansion outperforms other lanes with hard observed conversion data

### Audit conclusion

Confidence profile:

- practical confidence: high
- evidence rigor: medium

Translation:

This is probably the right operating move, but not because the system independently discovered it from a broad, external evidence base. It is mostly because the current corpus and company state make it the most operationally plausible answer.

## Lane 2: Healthcare / Pharma Training Growth

The live report ranks this lane as the strongest adjacent durable growth lane because:

- buyer logic is clearer than in several other categories
- internal fit is credible
- external market evidence is stronger and more concrete
- the lane supports recurring, compliance-linked, and operationally meaningful work

### What supports it

The strongest support comes from:

- `ext-002-healthcare-medtech-signals.md`
- `6-24-month-strategy.md`
- Groove Jones healthcare/pharma past-performance references

The external research is materially better here than for the top lane:

- verified GE HealthCare + MediView deployment signal

That means the lane is not only an internal theory. It is supported by real category evidence outside Groove Jones's own documents.

### What is weak

Weaknesses remain:

- the report does not fully quantify actual Groove Jones win probability, margin profile, or staffing readiness for a heavier healthcare push
- some report support still comes from strategic synthesis rather than raw operating metrics

### Audit conclusion

Confidence profile:

- practical confidence: high
- evidence rigor: medium-high

Translation:

If one lane in the current report is genuinely better supported by the evidence base, it is healthcare/pharma training.

## What I Trust Most In The Current Report

- the immediate call to stabilize through current-client expansion and support
- the medium-term call to build toward healthcare/pharma training

## What I Trust Less

## Final Audit View

The report is useful for decision-making, but it should be read as:

- a strong operating synthesis
- a moderate-quality evidence synthesis
- not a final independent proof engine

If leadership wants the safest interpretation:

- act on the recommendation
- but keep distinguishing between `operationally compelling` and `independently proven`
