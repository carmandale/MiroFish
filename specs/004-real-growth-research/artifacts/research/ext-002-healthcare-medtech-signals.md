## Findings

### 1. The healthcare XR story is no longer just pilot-stage visualization

GE HealthCare and MediView announced the first installation and clinical use of the OmnifyXR Interventional Suite at North Star Vascular and Interventional in Minneapolis on June 20, 2024. GE says the system combines holographic heads-up display, 3D anatomy visualization, advanced imaging, and remote collaboration inside live interventional workflow.

That is strong evidence of real-world deployment behavior, not just conference-demo language.

### 2. Medtech training spend is also showing up in physical education-center buildouts

Stryker's June 18, 2024 release about IRCAD North America is strategically important because it is not a single app announcement. It is a multi-year agreement to help create a surgical training center focused on robotics, medical virtual and augmented reality, surgical AI, and simulation training. The release says the center will attract thousands of medical professionals each year.

That suggests healthcare XR demand is expanding through training infrastructure and curriculum partnerships, not only device demos.

### 3. Mixed reality is entering live procedural use, not staying confined to pre-op planning

Stryker's January 10, 2024 announcement about Blueprint Mixed Reality Guidance reports the first shoulder arthroplasty surgeries completed with the system at Mayo Clinic and St. Joseph's Health Care London. This is useful because it shows mixed reality attached to a clinical precision workflow rather than a marketing or education-only experience.

## What This Means For Groove Jones

- Healthcare and medtech remain one of the strongest adjacent categories because the buyer logic is concrete: procedural confidence, clinician training, workflow efficiency, and better visualization.
- Groove Jones should treat medtech and clinical-training buyers as a serious long-horizon lane, but with evidence that can also support immediate BD targeting.
- The strongest positioning angle is not "we do XR." It is "we help training, education, and high-stakes procedural understanding become more effective and easier to scale."

## Limits

- These are category signals, not direct proof of accessible near-term Groove Jones deal volume.
- EXT-004 and EXT-006 are product-linked clinical examples; Groove Jones would still need account-specific proof that agencies or specialist studios can enter adjacent scopes.
- The evidence here is stronger for healthcare/medtech strategy than for immediate cash recovery.
