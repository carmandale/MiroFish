## Findings

### 1. The Army is actively operationalizing immersive training, not just researching it

The official PD SAI article from May 19, 2025 says the Army delivered the first Virtual Aerial Systems Trainer classroom to the 204th Military Intelligence Battalion at Fort Bliss. The article describes it as an operational, first-of-its-kind capability supporting ATHENA and HADES ISR platforms, with modular VR training intended to reduce dependency on scarce flight hours while improving fidelity, safety, and repeatability.

That is meaningful because it shows immersive training attached to readiness, not a speculative innovation lab.

### 2. Small-business entry paths are real

The official SBIR award page for the Joint Explosive Threat Training (JETT) Platform shows a 2025 Army Phase I award to Inhance Digital Corporation for an XR training system combining AR, VR, and MR for Explosive Ordnance Disposal training. The page explicitly describes the Army's need for a scalable, immersive training solution and the proposed use of open architectures such as OpenXR.

This matters for Groove Jones because it proves that small-business-accessible pathways exist for immersive training concepts, even when the end market often feels dominated by large primes.

### 3. Synthetic training remains part of the Army's broader modernization path

The Army operational-demonstration article on the Synthetic Training Environment shows the broader institutional direction: live, virtual, and gaming-style training systems remain active modernization priorities across multiple platforms and units.

## What This Means For Groove Jones

- Defense should stay in the strategy portfolio because the institutional demand signal is real and current.
- The near-term move is probably not "win a giant prime contract." It is to map Groove Jones past performance and partner posture against smaller entry points, consortium routes, and subcontract positions.
- This lane is evidence-backed, but the sales cycle and compliance burden make it less useful for immediate runway recovery than maintenance expansion or healthcare-adjacent work.

## Limits

- These sources prove demand and program activity, not Groove Jones fit on a specific solicitation.
- Defense remains a slower lane with more qualification friction than the internal data suggests the company can tolerate for short-term cash rescue.
- Use this evidence to support strategic lane selection and partner outreach, not as proof of immediate close probability.
