## Bottom Line

The most evidence-backed durable strategy is:

1. use maintenance/support and existing-account expansion to stabilize the base
2. concentrate long-horizon growth on healthcare / medtech training and procedural understanding
3. keep defense as a partner-led option lane, not the main operating thesis

This is not because other lanes are impossible. It is because these three moves are the best fit between Groove Jones's actual past performance and the strongest evidence in the current workspace. This strategy also assumes leadership addresses the current profitability problem identified in `qbo_08.md`; a growth thesis alone will not rescue a negative-margin base.

## Strongest Strategic Truths

### 1. Groove Jones already has proof in training, support, and high-stakes visualization

Internal evidence:
- `13-groove-jones-capabilities.md` shows strong work in `J&J Ottava`, `Amgen Imdelltra AVP`, Army `Disasterville`, `Ideal Industries VR Safety Training`, and maintenance/support.
- `fc_05.md` shows `J&J Ottava` still active at meaningful budget scale.

Interpretation:
- The company does not need to invent a new identity from scratch.
- The existing evidence already supports a positioning around training, enablement, and operational XR rather than only experiential novelty.

### 2. Healthcare / medtech is the cleanest adjacent growth lane

External evidence:
- `ext-002-healthcare-medtech-signals.md` shows official deployments and training-center investment from GE HealthCare, MediView, and Stryker.

Internal fit:
- Existing J&J and Amgen work provide past-performance credibility.

Strategic implication:
- Healthcare / medtech should be treated as the primary adjacent lane for 6-24 month growth because it combines stronger buyer logic with better Groove Jones fit than a cold start in a new category.

### 3. Maintenance/support is the bridge from services churn to something more durable

Internal evidence:
- `13-groove-jones-capabilities.md` shows maintenance/support has the highest win rate in the current portfolio.

Inference:
- The best path toward any eventual platform or subscription story is not a sudden product leap. It is a named support layer that deepens post-launch ownership and creates repeatable operational revenue.

Strategic implication:
- The Orchestrator or fleet-management thesis should be developed from real support and device-operations pain, not from abstract product ambition.

### 4. Defense is real, but it should be pursued with patience and partner logic

External evidence:
- `ext-003-defense-training-signals.md` shows current Army deployment and small-business-accessible SBIR activity.

Internal fit:
- `13-groove-jones-capabilities.md` shows Army past performance already exists.

Strategic implication:
- Defense belongs in the portfolio, but as a measured lane built through partner routes, small-business pathways, and selective capture, not as the sole answer to near-term revenue pressure.

## Recommended Strategic Shape

### Primary lane: healthcare / medtech training and procedural understanding

What to build toward:
- repeatable training and enablement offers
- clinical and medtech education experiences
- mixed-reality or spatial-visualization work where Groove Jones can point to current past performance

Why this lane wins:
- strongest combined internal + external evidence
- buyer logic tied to training quality, clinician readiness, and workflow value
- less dependent on pure campaign budgets than brand/event XR

### Bridge lane: support, maintenance, and device operations

What to build toward:
- named maintenance/support packages
- post-launch content refresh retainers
- device fleet operations, compatibility, and analytics review
- selective productization only after the service layer proves the recurring need

Why this lane matters:
- it is the most practical path out of episodic-project churn
- it monetizes a capability Groove Jones already wins

### Option lane: defense / government training

What to build toward:
- partner-first bid strategy
- small-business pathways and targeted capability statements
- selective pursuit of immersive-training opportunities where prior Army work matters

Why this lane stays secondary:
- strong category signal
- weaker short-term conversion certainty
- slower compliance and procurement cycle

## Lanes To Treat More Selectively

### Sports / events / experiential XR

Evidence status:
- There is still buyer activity in spec-009 carry-forward research, especially `q1_active_xr_spenders.md`.
- But in this workspace the external evidence for durable, repeatable profitability is weaker than the healthcare and support cases.

Recommendation:
- Keep this lane for profitable relationship-led work and scanner/activation differentiation.
- Do not make it the primary 6-24 month growth thesis.

### Broad AI / XR positioning

Evidence status:
- There are many interesting AI-plus-XR ideas in the spec-009 context docs.
- Most of that material is still `ai-mediated-context` or `mixed-needs-review`.

Recommendation:
- Use AI selectively where it strengthens training, digital humans, or operational support.
- Do not anchor strategy on broad "AI-native agency" claims until the evidence base is stronger.

## What Would Change My Mind

- A clean data set proving that existing-account expansion is not materially better than true net-new.
- Verified evidence that sports / experiential buyers are generating more durable recurring revenue than the current workspace suggests.
- A defense partner path with concrete, near-term, qualified opportunity timing.
- Better capacity data showing Groove Jones can staff a healthcare- or defense-heavy expansion without delivery strain.
