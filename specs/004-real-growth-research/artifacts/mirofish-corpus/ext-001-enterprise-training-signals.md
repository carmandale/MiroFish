## Findings

### 1. The strongest public ROI proof in this bundle is still training effectiveness, not hardware excitement

PwC's official soft-skills study remains one of the cleanest enterprise XR proofs in the workspace. On the official study page, PwC says VR learners completed the same inclusive-leadership training up to four times faster than classroom learners, still three times faster after accounting for headset onboarding, and were up to 275% more confident applying the training afterward.

This matters because it is not a vague "XR is the future" claim. It is a concrete enterprise-learning efficiency case that can support Groove Jones positioning around training, onboarding, and behavior-change experiences instead of novelty activations.

### 2. Large enterprises are using immersive environments for workforce onboarding and learning, not just demos

Accenture's official "Nth Floor" material describes the environment as the "largest enterprise VR implementation to date" and explicitly says new joiners experience `One Accenture Park`, a space used for onboarding, immersive showcases, and hands-on demonstrations.

The useful point here is not the metaverse branding. It is that a global services firm sees enough value in immersive onboarding and distributed learning to maintain a named internal platform around it. That is meaningful buyer-behavior evidence for workforce enablement use cases.

### 3. Major services firms are still allocating capital toward immersive-learning platforms

Accenture's June 2023 investment in Praxis Labs is older than the newest sources here, but it is still a relevant signal because the company described Praxis as a VR SaaS platform for developing workplace soft skills and inclusion outcomes. That supports the broader conclusion that immersive learning remains one of the few XR categories major enterprises continue to fund with serious intent.

## What This Means For Groove Jones

- Training and workforce enablement remain a stronger evidence-backed lane than open-ended brand-experience XR.
- The better pitch is operational value: faster readiness, safer rehearsal, higher confidence, and repeatable onboarding.
- For `30-90 day` work, this supports packaging existing Groove Jones training and maintenance capability as a repeatable offer instead of only custom project work.

## Limits

- EXT-002 is enterprise self-use, not buyer procurement of an outside XR agency.
- EXT-003 is an investment signal, not a deployment case study.
- These sources support the category and the buying logic; they do not prove Groove Jones can win a particular account without additional internal and account-level evidence.
